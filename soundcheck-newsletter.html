It’s been an exciting month. Thank you for being a part of our journey as we continue making music licensing faster, smarter, and more creative.

Recent licensing disputes, like the Zee Music–Nykaa case, have prompted more brands to take music licensing seriously and we’re seeing more teams turn to Hoopr to get it right from day one. That’s exactly the shift we’ve been working towards.
